Privacy Policy✕

Last updated: 2026-10-10

1. What we process
No account is required and quiz answers are computed in your browser. Our servers handle standard technical data (IP address, page, browser, timestamps) for security, rate limiting and aggregated, de-identified completion counts.

2. Cookies, analytics and ads
We use Google Analytics 4 for usage statistics and Google AdSense to show ads. These Google services may use cookies or device identifiers, including for personalized ads. No other advertising network is used.

3. Your choice
Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see an accept/reject choice before non-essential cookies are stored. Rejecting still shows non-personalized ads. Manage personalized ads at Google Ads Settings.

4. Children
Pages based on child-oriented franchises (e.g. Bluey, Disney, Minecraft, Roblox, Fortnite, My Little Pony) run no ads and no analytics. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

5. Trademarks
All quizzes are unofficial fan content and are not affiliated with any named brand, artist, studio or rights holder. "MBTI" is a trademark of The Myers & Briggs Foundation.
